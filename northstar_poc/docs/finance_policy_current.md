# Finance Reporting Policy — Current
Document ID: FIN-POL-2025-V2
Status: APPROVED — CURRENT
Effective date: 2025-01-01
Supersedes: FIN-POL-2024-V1

For FY2025 and later, default revenue reporting uses net revenue, defined in FIN-METRICS-2025. Use posted ledger transactions and their transaction dates. Exclude drafts and voids. Refunds and credits reduce revenue. Do not use a sales dashboard's gross sales figure as net revenue. If sources conflict, prefer the approved current policy and authoritative ledger, and disclose the conflict.
